Basil Home

Privacy policy · last updated 12 September 2026

Basil Home is a personal dashboard written and used by one person, Basil Toufexis. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app reads and edits the owner's own Google Calendar events so they can be viewed and changed from the dashboard. It requests only the Google Calendar scope.

Where data lives

Calendar data is fetched directly from Google to the owner's own computer and displayed there. Nothing is sent to any other server or third party, nothing is sold, and no analytics or advertising services are involved. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google account permissions.
